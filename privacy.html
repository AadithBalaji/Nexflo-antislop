Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Draft. This is a starting template and must be reviewed by a qualified attorney before launch.

NexFlo Cloud ("NexFlo", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect on nexflo.cloud and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us through forms: name, email address, company, role, product interest, estimated volumes, current EDI provider and any message you send.
  • Usage information collected automatically, such as pages visited, browser type and approximate location, through privacy-respecting analytics.

How we use it

  • To respond to your requests, provide quotes and schedule demos
  • To send early-access and product updates you signed up for
  • To understand how the site is used and improve it

We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us run the site and our business (for example, email and hosting providers), under contracts that limit their use of it, or when required by law.

Retention

We keep form submissions only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as required by law.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from emails at any time and ask us to access, correct or delete your information by writing to support@nexflo.cloud.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. See our Security page.
