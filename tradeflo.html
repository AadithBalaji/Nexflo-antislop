TradeFlo

EDI for manufacturers, distributors and retail suppliers. Receive orders, send invoices and ship notices, and keep every retail partner happy.

Get early accessRequest pricing
What it does

Retailer EDI, translated into something your systems understand

TradeFlo receives your partners' EDI documents, turns them into clean JSON or files your systems can use, and sends your responses back in the exact format each partner expects.

Who it's for

  • Manufacturers selling to big-box retailers
  • Distributors and wholesalers with many trading partners
  • Retail and e-commerce suppliers facing new EDI requirements
  • Teams outgrowing expensive legacy EDI providers
Example

A Walmart purchase order, start to finish

An 850 arrives from Walmart. TradeFlo validates it, sends the 997 acknowledgment, and hands your system clean JSON. When you ship, your 856 and 810 go back the same way.

Features

What's included

X12 and EDIFACT translation

Read and write the standards your retail and international partners use.

EDI to JSON

Work with clean, documented JSON instead of segments and delimiters.

SFTP

Drop and pick up files from a secure SFTP folder. No code required.

REST API

Send and receive documents programmatically and connect your ERP or storefront.

Trading partner management

Keep each partner's IDs, specs and connection settings in one place.

Transaction tracking

Follow every document from received to acknowledged, with full history.

Error alerts

Get notified about rejected or late documents before they become chargebacks.

Validation

Catch missing fields and bad values before a document leaves your hands.

Hands-on onboarding

We help you connect your first trading partner, directly.
